Data Deletion Request

Last updated: July 14, 2026

You have the right to permanently delete your Chatterly account and all data associated with it. When your data is deleted, the following are permanently removed:

  • Your account information (email, name, phone number)
  • Connected Instagram account information and access tokens
  • All automation rules and DM flows you created
  • Automation logs (comment/DM/trigger history)
  • Ongoing conversation records

Method 1: From inside the app (recommended)

Open the Chatterly app → Settings → Delete my account and data. Once you confirm, all your data is deleted immediately and permanently. This cannot be undone.

Method 2: Request by email

If you no longer have access to the app, you can write from your registered email address to leventselcuk1@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Your request will be processed within 30 days at the latest, after identity verification.

Removing only the Instagram connection

If you only want to remove your Instagram account connection while keeping your other data (rules, history), you can remove the Instagram connection separately in the app under Settings > Connected Accounts.

For more information, see the Privacy Policy.
